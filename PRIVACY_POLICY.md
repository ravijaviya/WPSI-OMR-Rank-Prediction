# Privacy Policy

**Effective Date:** August 28, 2026  
**Application:** Wireless PSI - OMR Evaluation & Analysis Portal
**Developer:** RJ

---

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the **Wireless PSI - OMR Evaluation & Analysis Portal** ("we", "our", or "the platform") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you access or interact with our application.

### 2. Information We Collect
We collect minimal and non-sensitive information necessary to deliver scoring, ranking, and analytics services:

* **Google Account Information (OAuth):** When you sign in with Google to access gated features, we access your primary email address and public basic profile name solely for identity verification and authorization checks.
* **Exam & Evaluation Data:** When submitting an OMR sheet for evaluation, we collect your 8-digit Examination Roll Number (starting with 300), Paper Set code, Gender, Category, and the extracted bubble response markings.
* **Beta Application Details:** If you apply for early access, we collect your full name, Google email address, and Roll Number to link and verify your account.
* **Diagnostic & Usage Metadata:** When downloading datasets, technical diagnostic metadata is recorded, including IP address, approximate geographic location (city, region, country, ISP), browser type, device type, and operating system.

### 3. How We Use Information
The collected information is used strictly to:
* Parse, evaluate, and score OMR response sheets against provisional and official answer keys.
* Calculate live rank leaderboards and simulate cut-off projections across vertical and horizontal reservations.
* Authenticate user access and authorize access to restricted beta features.
* Prevent automated abuse, monitor server stability, and maintain application integrity.

### 4. Data Sharing, Storage & Masking
* **Public Visibility:** Roll Numbers displayed on public leaderboard tables are masked (e.g., `30****45`) to safeguard candidate identity.
* **No Sensitive Personal Information:** We do not request, store, or process sensitive personal data such as passwords, OTPs, mobile phone numbers, or government-issued identification cards.
* **Third-Party Data Storage:** Application records and logs are securely maintained through Google Cloud Platform APIs and Google Sheets infrastructure.
* **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties or advertising networks.

### 5. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance
Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any revisions will be reflected with an updated effective date at the top of this document.

### 7. Contact Information
If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the project repository or submit a note via the in-app support channel.
