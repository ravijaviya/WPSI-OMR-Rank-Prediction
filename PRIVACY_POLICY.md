# Privacy Policy

**Application:** Wireless PSI - OMR Evaluation & Analysis Portal 
**Developer:** RJ 

---

### 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how the **Wireless PSI - OMR Evaluation & Analysis Portal** ("we", "our", or "the platform") collects, uses, processes, and protects your information when you access or interact with our application.

### 2. Information We Collect
We collect minimal and non-sensitive information strictly necessary to deliver scoring, ranking, and analytics services:

* **Google Account Information (OAuth):** When you sign in with Google to access gated features, we access your primary email address and public basic profile name solely for identity verification and authorization checks.
* **Exam & Demographic Data:** Roll Number, Gender, Category (GEN, EWS, OBC, SC, ST), Age, and eligibility for sports/widow quotas.
* **Submission Data (No OMR File Storage):** We strictly **do not store or retain your uploaded OMR PDF or image files**. Once your uploaded document is processed in-memory to extract bubble coordinates and markings, the file is immediately discarded. We only store the extracted raw responses (e.g., marked answers, paper code, and computed section scores) exactly as displayed inside your dashboard.
* **Payment Verification Data:** When verifying premium access via UPI, we process the transaction note / reference and payment amount to confirm activation. We do not collect or store bank account numbers, UPI PINs, or card details.
* **Diagnostic & Usage Metadata:** Technical diagnostic metadata is recorded, including IP address, approximate geographic location (city, region, country, ISP), browser type, device type, and operating system to prevent abuse, enforce rate limits, and maintain server stability.

### 3. What We Do NOT Collect
* **No Sensitive Personal Information:** We do not request, store, or process sensitive personal data such as passwords, OTPs, mobile phone numbers, credit/debit card numbers, or government-issued identification cards.

### 4. How We Use Information
The collected information is used strictly to:
* Parse, evaluate, and score responses against provisional and final answer keys.
* Calculate real-time rank leaderboards and simulate cut-off projections across vertical and horizontal quota rules.
* Authenticate user identity and restrict access to verified/premium tools.
* Prevent automated scraping, enforce fair usage, and ensure portal stability.

### 5. Data Sharing, Storage & Masking
* **Public Visibility:** Roll numbers displayed on public leaderboards and recent submission widgets are permanently masked (e.g., `30****10`) to safeguard candidate identity.
* **Third-Party Infrastructure:** Application data is stored and processed securely using Neon PostgreSQL database instances and Google Cloud APIs.
* **No Sale of Data:** We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties or advertising networks.

### 6. Service Lifespan & Automatic Data Deletion
* **Temporary Analytic Service:** This portal operates exclusively as a temporary predictive tool.
* **Permanent Closure & Purge:** The portal will be **permanently taken offline and shut down within 3 days of the official declaration of provisional results by the GPRB**. Upon closure, all stored candidate records, account associations, scores, and analytics databases will be permanently destroyed.

### 7. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance
Our use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adhere strictly to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
